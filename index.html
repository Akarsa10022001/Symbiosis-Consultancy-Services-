    Students ask

    Straight answers before you pay anything

    Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp, or DM @symbiosis.consultancy.

    NEET UG required for MBBS, BDS & AYUSH
    Do I have to pay a donation or capitation fee?

    No. We guide direct admissions with no donation and no middlemen. Fees follow the institution's regulated fee structure.*

    Who do I pay the course fees to?

    Directly to the college or university. You get the institution's receipt, not ours.

    What is the one-time payment (OTP)?

    A one-time admission registration, paid with your documents to confirm your seat. Depending on the programme it is ₹5,000 or ₹10,000. Each course card above shows the exact amount.

    Is NEET required?

    NEET UG is required for MBBS, BDS and AYUSH (BAMS, BHMS, BUMS). For other healthcare programmes, eligibility and NEET rules vary by university. We verify them for you before you pay anything.

    Which documents do I need?

    10th and 12th marksheets & certificates, graduation marksheets (as per course), caste / category certificate if applicable, ID proof (Aadhaar / PAN / Voter ID) and 2–4 recent passport-size photographs.

    I'm working. Can I still get a degree?

    Yes. We guide you to UGC-recognised universities with flexible study options and valid degrees, plus an Executive MBA for professionals with 5+ years of experience. Distance options are available in some courses.

    Are there charges beyond tuition?

    Exam fees, certificates, hostel and mess are usually separate. Where a programme has a consultancy & document-handling charge (for example ₹25,000 for BHMS Bhopal), we show it upfront on the course card.
